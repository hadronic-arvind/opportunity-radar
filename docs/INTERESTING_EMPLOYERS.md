# Distinctive employer coverage

The shared catalog includes 48 employers across 49 official resources, bringing the catalog to 401 resources.
All 48 companies are searchable in the main discovery view and resource library, including companies with no matching openings.
Company matches link to the official board and show whether automatic collection is available.
Automatic and named coverage include the distinctive-employer pack for every profile.
Individual jobs are ranked against each profile; employer industries do not exclude unrelated occupations.
Manual coverage and explicit source exclusions remain authoritative.

## Collection status

38 employers have automatic listing collectors; 10 remain clearly labeled manual resources.
Bose uses the public Workday board, rather than its JavaScript careers landing page.
Structured collectors check complete pagination before publishing a source snapshot.
Workday and SmartRecruiters collect search results first, then enrich descriptions within a bounded time and byte budget.
Listings without available descriptions retain explicit title-only provenance; they do not invent qualification evidence.
HTML feeds are experimental and bounded by configured pages; they can miss future expansion beyond that bound.
Company tags describe career areas, not a promise of a vacancy or a verified hiring frequency.

The remaining manual sites require further provider work or currently reject public requests.
They are not presented as functioning automatic feeds.
Manual employers: Dolby, HARMAN, Bang & Olufsen, MathWorks, Dassault Systemes, Rohde & Schwarz, Hamamatsu Photonics, DJI, Universal Robots, TAE Technologies.

# Distinctive employer coverage

Added 48 official employer resources on 2026-10-10, bringing the public catalog to 400 resources.
The selection spans audio, scientific computing, precision instruments, product design, robotics, quantum technology, and materials or energy.
Hiring frequency is not verified or used as a catalog label.
Role and field tags describe career areas to explore, not a promise that a matching vacancy is currently open.

All profiles can search these resources by company, field, role family, opportunity type, or career stage in the resource library.
The `interesting-employers` pack offers the complete selection.
Five supported structured feeds join shared cross-industry coverage; two HTML link feeds are enabled through their relevant selected packs.
The remaining 41 pages are explicitly manual resources, remain visible even when disabled, and never masquerade as job listings.
Bose Corporation is a manual resource because its JavaScript careers page exposes no job anchors to the supported link collector.
Bose Professional is a separate employer resource with an experimental, title-only feed.
Experimental HTML feeds can miss pagination and lack full qualification evidence, so users must review the official listing.

## Verification

Official careers pages were checked by bounded HTTPS requests or official web results when direct requests were blocked.
Supported and experimental feeds were exercised through the production adapters without personal state.
Observed listing counts: Kitware 19, Helion Energy 69, QuEra Computing 75, Form Energy 205, Formlabs 237, Bose Professional 11, COMSOL 23.
Counts change over time and include all entries published by the employer board.
Direct requests to D-Wave and Skydio candidate ATS boards failed; their verified careers portals remain manual.
